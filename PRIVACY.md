# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

LeoChat (and the personal assistant built on it) is a personal, self-hosted tool. It is run by its author on the author's own computer, for the author's own use. It is not offered as a service to other people.

## What the app accesses

When the author signs in with Google, the app may request access to:

- **Gmail** – to read and summarise the author's own mail and, only after the author approves each message, to send mail on the author's behalf.
- **Google Calendar** – to read the author's schedule and add events to the author's own calendar.
- **Google Drive** – to read files the author chooses to work with.

## How the data is used

- Data is processed only to provide these features to the author.
- Google data is stored locally on the author's own machine. It is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising.
- To generate a reply, the relevant text may be sent to the large-language-model provider the author has configured. Nothing else leaves the machine.
- The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>. Deleting the app's local data folder removes everything it has stored.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at <https://github.com/cybermanhao/LeoChat/issues>.
